Meet the salesperson

Casey Compton sells solutions, not just products

Casey works with business customers to find the technology that solves their problems. I interviewed him (he’s my dad) about how his job works and how fast-moving technology, especially AI, is changing it.

His answer surprised me. New tools make some tasks easier, but the heart of the job is still listening to people and solving their problems. The job takes two skill sets at once: deep technical knowledge and real people skills.

“No two days are ever the same.”Casey Compton, on why he likes the job

Want in?

What to bring to a tech sales job

Casey says employers look for more than a persuasive personality. Salespeople have to understand what they sell and explain how it meets a customer’s needs.

Strong tech background Experience in the field Basic math Microsoft Office A habit of learning
The hardest part

You have to “constantly keep up and stay informed about new developments.”

Security, how customers plan to use new tools, and now AI all keep changing. Learning the product once isn’t enough.

The bottom line

Technology changes how the job gets done. Understanding people is still the job.

Even Casey’s trips to NBA, MLB and NFL games with customers are about relationships, and he admits even that gets old. Tools will keep getting faster. Flexibility, constant learning and working well with people are what make someone good at this career.
